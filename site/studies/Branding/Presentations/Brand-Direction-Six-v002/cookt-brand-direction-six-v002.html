06 / Brand direction study. In review.

Source and system notes

Oversized fork, pasta and bowl shapes connect illustration, food photography and print. Each application changes its composition and scale. Echo, NB International, the current house palette and original retail fronts remain the anchors.

Presentation sequence informed by Barsk by Heavy. COOKT identity and imagery are separate work. Existing photographic concepts are reused; the flat food illustration and physical range/print scenes are generated concepts. Exact retail artwork is shown in its dedicated proof. Generated packaging surfaces and small print are illustrative, not production artwork.

Food claims, dimensions, materials and production approval remain open. This study has not been published.
