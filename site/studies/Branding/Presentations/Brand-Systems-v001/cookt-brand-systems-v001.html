Three brand systems

COOKT / Three brand systems

Three visual-system proposals using the selected Echo logo. Pack fronts preserve the existing design and dish colors, with the Echo substitution already present in the source review artwork. New applications are design concepts.

Typography: supplied NB International Pro Regular, Bold and Mono. House colors: mint #DCEBD5, deep green #194B39, paprika #9B2D1F and cream #F6EFE4. The existing dish palette remains separate. No new pack color is proposed.

Art direction reference: Julia Compton / plates. The Menu draws on the board’s typographic packaging and ordering systems; Market Editions on produce prints and illustrated food ephemera; Table Studies on composed dining scenes and ceramic color. Reference artwork is not reproduced in this deck.

Market illustration: original generated concept artwork. Existing lifestyle photographs are art-direction studies; source pack food imagery is retained. None of these images verifies a final recipe, portion or manufactured object.

Design review only. Existing protein, certification, origin and net-weight copy remains subject to product-specific verification. The locked brand line is A FRESH TAKE ON FROZEN.
