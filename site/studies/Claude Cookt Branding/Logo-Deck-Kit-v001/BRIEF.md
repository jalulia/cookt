# COOKT logo deck — brief for Astra (v001, 6 Oct 2026)

Claude drew the marks and owns narrative, copy and final QA. Astra builds the deck, renders and mockups. Julia approves.

## The job
A client-facing logo presentation for Azir (COOKT). Visual first. One idea per slide. Short lines, never paragraphs. No bullet lists, no cards, no dense grids. It should feel like a confident type foundry specimen crossed with a food brand.

## Format
- HTML deck, 16:9 at 1920×1080, arrow-key navigation, plus a PDF export.
- Build in `Branding/Presentations/Logo-Deck-v001/`. Read assets from this kit; copy what you use into the deck folder.

## Look
- Ground: deep green `#103E2C` and cream `#F5EDD9`. Dish fields only where packs appear (Rasta `#112113`, Blackened `#C93D27`, Orzo `#FFECA4`, Chipotle `#351114`).
- The wordmark is the only display type. Supporting text: one clean grotesk, two sizes (headline ~56–72 px, caption ~20 px). No serif display face, no italics, no letterspaced all-caps headlines beyond small labels.
- Generous margins (≥120 px). Let the marks run big, even bleed off the edge.
- Never: walls of text, drop shadows, gradients, icons, emoji, stock lifestyle imagery, "Town & Country" tablescapes, stripes or plaid.
- US English. No slogans or aphorisms. Copy below is final unless Claude changes it.
