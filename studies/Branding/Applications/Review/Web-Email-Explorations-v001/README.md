The four older pack faces are replaced with the exact Artwork-v014 fronts, homography-fitted to each carton; carton edges recolored to the new field colors. Tile, towel, light and shadows are the original photograph.
- Tile pair: left half is the photograph above; right of the grout line at x=776 the tile is rebuilt from profiles measured across the photograph's own grout, with Overhead-v001 Rasta and Orzo plates and continued carton shadows.
- Mint tile: the same two images re-glazed (tile to mint, grout to deep green); cartons, plates and towel are masked and untouched.
- All other photographs are existing project files (Curated-v001, Direction-v002/v003, Render-v014). None of these are approved production photography.

Copy uses only canonical dish names and descriptions from brand.json, the supplied wireframe text and the current DTC ordering facts (7 to 30 meals; weekly, every two weeks, monthly or one-time). No new product claims.
