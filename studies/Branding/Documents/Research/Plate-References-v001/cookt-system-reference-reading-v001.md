# COOKT / Reading the supplied reference board

Read visually from all four saved board screenshots, with the pin register used only to locate links. Pinterest titles identify the visible exemplars; uncertain creator credits are not inferred. These references show a designed collection of objects, images and graphic surfaces—not a requirement to manufacture a branded restaurant or put ingredients around every rim.

## 1. A table is composed from different visual frequencies

[Hokolo’s four settings](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551821/) alternate solid table grounds, patterned rectangular mats, circular plate borders and small plain accessories. The [mixed tabletop](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551822/) deliberately opposes long striped runners, small repeating marks and round dishes. Different objects do different jobs. Our proposals repeated the same mint field, paprika interruption and isolated ingredient across every surface. Even the penne rim treated repetition as the concept. Transfer the relationship between coarse, medium and quiet areas; do not copy Hokolo’s circles, colors or specific patterns.

## 2. Illustration can depict a complete scene—not merely decorate an object

The visible “Tomato Pasta – Asahi…” image stages chair slats, checked cloth, bottle, plate, fork and food as one authored perspective. The [Thailand table illustration](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551812/) builds appetite through overlapping dishes, glasses and oblique viewpoints. The [fish platter illustration](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551813/) makes the fish, herbs and oval border one image. Our isolated painted lemon enlarged beside type, and then enlarged on a plate, never developed an illustrative composition. The transferable behavior is deliberate relationships and overlap within a complete picture. Copying these artists’ fish, perspective distortions or brush signatures would miss that lesson.

## 3. The graphic belongs to the object’s construction

[BETTR’s yellow illustrated tape](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551867/) is a narrow repeated element that actually closes the cartons; the large black identity occupies the remaining field. [Leibniz’s biscuit-like panels](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551865/) turn product shape into label framing and alternate it with oversized type. [Sobo’s plated food](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551847/) gives borders, food and chopsticks separate spatial roles. Our arbitrary lid placement and mathematical rim annulus were applications without enough object-specific judgment. Transfer the idea that folds, closures, edges and handling determine graphic position—not the competitors’ tape drawings or scallops.

## 4. Type and image change each other’s composition

The visible Chez Saint Pierre poster places small ingredient images inside large editorial lines. [Chobani’s fruit emblem](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551833/) lets lettering occupy the ingredient’s shape. [Chicken Bites Break](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551866/) tightly negotiates tall type and little figures across stacked faces. Our type and illustration were adjacent independent layers, often competing at equal weight. Transfer planned intersections, purposeful scale inequality and lettering fitted to available space. Keep NB International and the exact Echo geometry; copying another brand’s novelty lettering is unnecessary.

## 5. A collection shares discipline without repeating one layout

The [four ceramic plates](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551801/) relate through confident line, limited accents and visible ceramic space, while using an eye, stripes, a heart and a wavy edge differently. The [decorative plate collection](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/2392606049551809/) varies border density and interval instead of stamping one motif onto every dish. Our penne diagram was evenly repeated clip-art; the botanical lemon was traditional engraving; neither established a deliberate family. Transfer controlled differences in density, spacing and subject. Do not lift the eye/heart symbols or convert the collection into five arbitrary COOKT icons.

**The failure was compositional authorship, not insufficient decoration.** These references make clear decisions about what dominates, what supports it, how objects meet, and where imagery stops. More motifs, more mockups, or a different rendering engine would not repair our current work.
