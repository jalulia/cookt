# COOKT / Brand design brief

## Objective

Build a complete COOKT brand world that feels hot, fresh, contemporary and culturally sharp, presented with the craft and coherence of a high-end design studio. The design needs a recognizable point of view, deliberate relationships between its components, and enough range to work in the real world.

## Scope

The original request was three different brand systems. The user clarified that the presentation overall needed stronger reference understanding. Subsequent work expanded into six directions; the active request became a whole new direction 06. Multiple replacement 06 attempts were rejected. This handoff asks for a new complete 06, within that wider presentation context. Directions 01–05 are included as comparison thumbnails, not approved templates.

## Fixed starting points

- Use the exact latest selected COOKT logo, Echo (option B). Use the supplied native SVG geometry; do not approximate it with a font or ask an image generator to redesign it.
- Preserve the existing pack designs and pack colors. On packaging, swap only the logo. Retain front proportions, typography, bowl placement, footer and flavor separation.
- Locked brand line: **A FRESH TAKE ON FROZEN**. Sentence case: **A fresh take on frozen**.
- American English. Product names: Jamaican Rasta Chicken Pasta; Creamy Blackened Chicken Pasta; Lemon Chicken Orzo; Chipotle Chicken with Corn. Spell out “with Corn.”

## Creative task

Develop a recognizable visual language around those assets: typography, composition, graphic elements, photography, color relationships and materials. Give it enough range for different, purposeful applications. A system must explain how these elements work together; repeating the same headline and image across formats does not establish one.

Make food photography central: appetizing dishes, close details, people eating and convincing photographs of the actual cartons. Show photographic and graphic layers working together. The system must extend beyond the food photograph itself.

Illustration is optional. If used, give it a distinct supporting role and a contemporary sensibility. No illustration style has been selected in this conversation. Neither the fine-line serving drawing nor its large fork/pasta replacement was accepted.

The project record identifies mint as primary and paprika as secondary. Supporting brand color refinement remains open; the existing pack colors are fixed for this task. The older numeric palette included with the exact identity files is provenance, not permission to repeat the rejected muted aesthetic. International font files are supplied as existing resources; this brief does not lock every typography choice from prior executions.

## Reference roles

- **Pinterest / Plates:** https://www.pinterest.com/juliacompton/plates/ — visual taste, composition, food/plate/graphic relationships and color. Inspect the actual saved references and live board; do not rely on earlier assistant interpretations.
- **Barsk by Heavy:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/253206713/Barsk — the user's explicit standard for presenting a complete brand. Study its identity coherence, physical applications, depth, detail, changing scale and image-led pacing. COOKT needs its own identity.

## Required presentation

A well-designed, immersive case study demonstrating the full system: rich packaging scenes, food photography, print, campaign and digital applications, with details that make the design logic visible. Show believable objects, useful close-ups and varied compositions. The narrative should help a viewer understand the whole brand rather than assemble a mood board of disconnected treatments.

Supply editable native layout/source assets and a reviewable PDF or equivalent presentation. Generated scenes may be used as concepts, but exact original pack artwork must also be visible and available. Do not present generated logo/pack text approximations as exact production work.

## Rejected characteristics

Nostalgic restaurant aesthetics; dainty editorial drawings; French/bistro character; stuffy restraint; dull color relationships; flat template layouts; repetitive tagline compositions; arbitrary box constructions; decorative substitutions for photography; and cosmetic restyling called a new system.

## Open production facts

Product claims, supplier dimensions, materials, print proofs and final photography remain open. Preserve supplied review copy. Do not invent nutrition, net weight, recipes, preparation instructions, certifier approval or origin evidence. Mockup protein values and generic halal artwork are design-review content, not substantiated claims. Existing net-weight placeholders remain placeholders. Carton front proportions and relative source geometry are supplied; actual physical dimensions are unverified.

Nothing in this package approves or publishes a new creative direction.
