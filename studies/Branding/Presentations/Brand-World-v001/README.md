Five-board internal direction proof. This is not a completed or approved visual system.

Open `cookt-brand-world-v001.html`. Arrow keys navigate; Home/End jump; F enters full screen. PDF, all-slide PNGs and the contact sheet are in `output/` and `review/`. The PDF is 2.4 MB; the native PDF source is retained separately.

## What this tests

The menu gives the range a clear reading order. NB International Pro Regular carries full dish names, Mono provides dish indices, and the exact Echo wordmark remains the strongest typographic weight. A dish index links the menu to a place marker. The existing painted lemon becomes the entire surface of a picture plate; eating would progressively reveal it. That is a proposed dining interaction, not a production or commercial claim. The campaign uses the same ingredient at a different distance. The delivery board preserves the supplied shipper art; it is a continuity reference, not newly designed packaging.

## Sources and limits

- Fonts: local NB International Pro Regular, Bold and Mono Regular, verified from font metadata.
- Logo: exact supplied Echo paths, fill recolor only.
- Lemon: existing `Claude Cookt Branding/Brand-Identity-Deck-v003/painted/cookt-painted-lemon-v001.png`, unchanged texture mapped into a native Blender plate.
- Shipper: corrected v002 exact-art render, source artwork unchanged.
- Plate: modeled material concept, not a manufactured object or food-contact approval.
- No generated food photographs, external reference images, scalloped plate, orange strokes, nested bowl, botanical vector plate or penne study enter these five boards.

The full visual system, food photography and bespoke illustration direction remain unresolved. The picture plate must be judged as an object and across more than one dish before becoming a brand rule. Do not promote this proof into living guidelines or client exports.
