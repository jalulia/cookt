- Pages 10–16: Market Editions. Original illustration, bold type and changing image scale.
- Pages 17–23: Table Studies. Editorial photography, quiet margins and scene-to-detail rhythm.
- Pages 1–2 establish the shared identity and pack range. Page 24 compares the directions.
