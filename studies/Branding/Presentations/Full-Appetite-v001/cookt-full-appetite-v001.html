06 / Full Appetite

06 / Full Appetite

A new direction 06, replacing Dish Papers. Food photography leads; Echo, mint, strong crops and a small painted accent connect the system.

Original Echo pack-front JPEGs are mapped unchanged onto native 3D cartons. Shippers, carton side surfaces, materials and relative dimensions are presentation concepts. These are renders, not photographs of manufactured packaging.

Food and lifestyle photographs are reused from the existing COOKT image library; lifestyle images remain art-direction concepts. The painted pepper is an existing study. No food image or pack front was regenerated for this direction.

Typography: supplied NB International Pro. The selected Echo SVG geometry is retained. House colors: mint #DCEBD5, green #194B39; paprika and yellow appear through the existing dish palette and imagery.

Inspiration: plates board. Physical object hierarchy, varying image scale and purposeful overlaps inform the compositions. Existing pack claims remain in review. A FRESH TAKE ON FROZEN is retained.
