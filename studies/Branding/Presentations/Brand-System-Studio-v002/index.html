Review notes

Current front geometry
Supplied Artwork-v014 PNGs and vector PDFs are the exact latest fronts. Current alternatives and DTC with/without bowl come from Front-Options-v001, edited directly from the exact v014 PDFs. Earlier retail v012 and DTC v013 sources remain available separately as historical review variants. The supplied fronts have not been recomposed.

System details
A fresh take on frozen. Full-height exact wordmark. Matched 1.4:1 front proportions. USA–halal–48g badge. Product-name weight comparison. Exact Rafiz green Rasta, green-rim Blackened, solid cobalt-rim Orzo and red-rim Chipotle bowls. Compact bottom-row heat: Rasta 3, Blackened 2, Chipotle 1; none on Orzo.

Back panels
Current retail backs, top panels and right-side continuations are linked from Dielines-v001. Rasta and Orzo have source mechanical PDFs; Blackened and Chipotle back panels remain design proofs. Earlier panel comparisons stay in the earlier review family.

Production fields
Preparation text is working copy. Nutrition values, approved ingredient and allergen declarations, net weight, barcode, manufacturer details, storage and supplier dielines still need final artwork inputs. Missing fields are visibly marked; no product facts have been invented.

Photography & brand worlds
New generated campaign studies. D1 and D2 are design proposals. The type and color controls are comparison tools and do not change the source brand record.
